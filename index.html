ABOUT US

Boro Fights Cancer (BFC) raises money to help fight cancer on several occasions throughout the year through fundraisers such as selling socks, shirts, stickers, and masks. BFC is a strong club at the FHS because of its constant efforts to help fight against cancer, support treatments, and boost funds towards research.

LANYARDS

BFC is selling lanyards for $5 in purple or pink. All profits go toward cancer research, so every purchase helps make a difference. The lanyards are a great way to show your Boro pride, support the cause, and add some color to your keys.

New drivers can use a Boro Fights Cancer lanyard to keep their keys handy, while Boro students can attach their student IDs and always have them with them.

Look out for BFC members and buy a lanyard for $5 to show your support!
